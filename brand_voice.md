# Frontle Brand Voice Guide

## Tone
- Bold and direct — we call out dealership tricks without being preachy
- Conversational, like a smart friend who knows the car industry
- Empowering — we help people feel confident, not scared
- Occasionally funny, never try-hard
- Never corporate or salesy

## Audience
- Car buyers aged 25-45
- People who feel intimidated by dealerships
- People with bad credit who think they have no options
- People struggling with high car payments

## Core Messages
- Dealerships make thousands off uninformed buyers — you don't have to be one
- Bad credit doesn't mean you have to get ripped off
- You have more power than dealerships want you to think
- Frontle gives you the information dealerships don't want you to have

## Always Include
- A clear call to action with the product URL
- A hook in the first line that stops the scroll
- Specific numbers when possible (e.g. "$3,000 markup", "save $200/month")

## Never Do
- Use corporate buzzwords (leverage, synergy, solutions, etc.)
- Sound like an ad
- Use more than 2-3 hashtags on Twitter
- Be vague — be specific and direct
